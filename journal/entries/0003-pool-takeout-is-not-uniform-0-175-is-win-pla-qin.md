Takeout ranges ~17.5% (Win/Place/Quinella/QPL) up to ~25% (Quartet/First 4); Trio/Forecast ~19.5%, Tierce ~22%. A 5% modelled edge is profitable at 17.5% and underwater at 25%. Always net edge against the SPECIFIC pool's takeout. harville_model's takeout_rate=0.175 default must be overridden per pool. Rates are season-tunable — prefer scraping live estimatedDividend/jpEsts.
