FINAL — the Wave-1 conclusion. The causal rebuild (thread 0046) bet that stripping the meta-signals
and identifying ability from first-principles physics on the race PATH would let the meta-signals
(form/jockey/collateral) RE-EMERGE as consequences. Wave 1 tested that bet to the bottom. It does
not hold at the win gate for the longitudinal path. Here is the whole chain and the reframe.

## The verdict — the strict re-emergence thesis is FALSIFIED at the win gate

The longitudinal sectional path is a **structurally weaker ability instrument than the form
aggregate.** Even granting a perfect, maximally-form-aligned extraction of everything the path
carries (the oracle), it converts to ~27–32% of market — under half of what a single form strength
already delivers. The reason is fundamental: **form is a multi-race OUTCOME aggregate** — it
integrates everything that determined past finishes, including the physiology the path shows AND the
deployment, traffic, intent, and condition it does not. Single/career-aggregated path KINEMATICS are
a partial, noisier projection of the same latent ability. The deleted model's 65% lived in the
multi-signal STACK + market-calibrated couplings, and no single physics ability read replaces it.

This is not a failure of execution — the physics is sound, the EOM constants are physiological, the
identification is honest, collateral did weakly re-emerge (0049). It is a falsification of the
strong form of the thesis: *you cannot recover form-level win skill from longitudinal-path physics
alone.* The discipline worked — it told us the truth before we built a generative-core rebuild that
the exploitation ceiling (0057) shows would have topped out near ~30%.

## The reframe — two honest routes forward (Wave 2 candidates, not mutually exclusive)

1. **NEW ORTHOGONAL DATA — the CV lateral path (the live frontier).** The ~27% ceiling is
   LONGITUDINAL-only. The lateral path (rail-offset position, control/ground-saved, traffic, draft)
   carries signal that is NOT in the longitudinal sectionals AND that form CANNOT see (form
   integrates outcomes, not within-race geometry/control). This is the one place physics can ADD
   over market+form. The CV pipeline prototype works (0055) and the re-id OCR is now solved
   (RapidOCR, 0058) — S0b → S8 (control latent → jockey re-emergence) → S9 (C_dA / draft /
   geometry×control) is the route to test whether lateral data lifts the ceiling.

2. **FORM-AS-MEASUREMENT (a thesis refinement, not a regression epicycle).** The strict ban on
   meta-signals was a methodological device to force re-emergence; the data shows re-emergence
   fails. The causally-honest resolution: a horse's past finishes/margins ARE legitimate
   OBSERVATIONS of its latent ability — a measurement of the consequence. A generative latent-state
   model should FUSE all observations of the latent (path kinematics + past results/form + trials)
   through a measurement model, with the physics as the prior/dynamics. This re-admits the strong
   signal CAUSALLY (form as noisy data about the latent, not a gain tuned to fit wins — which is
   what the deleted acausal model did and why it was purged). This is the Bayesian-coherent path
   back to competitive skill, and it is a thesis-level decision (it reverses the 0046 strip mandate).

## Disposition of the Wave-1 plan
- **DONE / decisive:** S1, S1b, S1c, S1d (the ability-read investigation), S4 (multi-pool), S0
  (CV prototype), S0b-OCR (RapidOCR chosen + `read_legend` wired).
- **SUPERSEDED by the verdict (not built):** the multi-section propagation rebuild and its feeders
  as a *market-competitive* effort (S2 field→drift, S3 margins, S5 retest, S6 P_max/τ, S7
  equilibrium-pace). They remain valid model-QUALITY / path-fit work but are no longer the path to
  win skill; revisit only under a chosen Wave-2 route.
- **The live frontier:** S0b (full CV re-id + backfill) → S8 → S9.

The path-physics model's defensible standing: a physically-grounded, leak-free, diagnostic-strong
model that tops out ~30% of market on longitudinal data alone — a component and a measurement
instrument, not a standalone market-beater. Where it goes next is the Wave-2 fork above.
