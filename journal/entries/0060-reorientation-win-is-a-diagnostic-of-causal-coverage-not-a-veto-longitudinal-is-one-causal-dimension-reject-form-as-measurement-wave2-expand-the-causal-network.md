FINAL — a course correction on the INTERPRETATION of Wave 1 (the data in 0050–0059 is correct and
stands; the conclusion I drew from it was not). Direction from the project owner.

## The error in 0059's framing

0059 read the win-gate ceiling (longitudinal-path ability → ~27% ST / 32% HV of market, vs form 60%/
45%) as a near-FALSIFICATION of the causal thesis, and floated re-admitting form as a "measurement."
That inverts the philosophy. Two corrections:

1. **WIN% is a DIAGNOSTIC, not a veto.** It measures how much of the causal network we have built so
   far. It is NOT authorized to reject first-principles causal modelling. A low win number means
   "few causal dimensions modelled yet," not "the approach is wrong."

2. **Demanding that longitudinal-path physics ALONE match form is the wrong expectation.** Form is a
   multi-race OUTCOME aggregate that integrates MANY causal dimensions — physiology AND jockey
   control AND trainer prep AND condition AND deployment/field — exactly as `docs/THEORY.md` lays
   out. The longitudinal path is ONE dimension (physiology). Of course one dimension underperforms
   the all-dimension aggregate. That is expected, not a failure.

**Therefore form-as-measurement (0059 Route 2) is REJECTED.** Re-admitting form/jockey/collateral —
even dressed as a "measurement of the latent" — is regressing onto the correlation PROXY of the
underlying causations we are here to model. It is the acausal shortcut we deleted. The whole point is
to model the CAUSES form is a shadow of, so the meta-signals re-emerge as consequences of the full
causal network.

## The correct diagnosis

Longitudinal-path physics is **not the full causal picture** — it is the first dimension of it. The
~27% is the **diagnostic floor of single-dimension (physiology) causal coverage**, a progress marker
to build UP from by adding causal structure — never by adding the proxy. Re-emergence is not
expected from one dimension; it is the eventual signature of a sufficiently complete causal network.

## Wave 2 — EXPAND THE CAUSAL NETWORK (the dimensions form aggregates, each from its OWN surface)

WIN diagnoses cumulative coverage; each dimension also earns its OWN re-emergence test. None re-admit
a meta-signal as an input.

- **CONTROL cause (jockey)** ← the CV LATERAL path (rail-offset position, ground-saved, traffic
  navigation, draft). Orthogonal signal NOT in the longitudinal sectionals and invisible to form's
  aggregate-of-outcomes. Re-emergence test: does JOCKEY skill re-emerge from the control latent?
  Route: S0b (CV re-id + backfill; OCR solved → RapidOCR, 0058) → S8 → S9. **The frontier.**
- **FIELD / DEPLOYMENT cause** ← the equilibrium pace / mean-field game over the field's running-style
  composition (`run_policy_dataset`, data-ready) + wiring the field/pace coupling into the generative
  dynamics. Causal (game-theoretic pace + draft), philosophy-pure. (The former "S2/S7" — NOT
  superseded; this is a genuine causal dimension.)
- **PREP cause (fitness on the day)** ← training-load / trackwork (Banister), modelled CAUSALLY as a
  modulation of the physiological constants (CP/W' on the day), NOT as the deleted gallop-fusion
  regression feature.
- **CONDITION cause (soundness)** ← gait CV (gated on parade footage).
- **PHYSIOLOGY cause** ← longitudinal path (Wave 1; the ~27% baseline). Sharpening levers (margins→W',
  P_max/τ) remain valid physiology-dimension work, not a win-skill dead end.

## Disposition correction
The 0059 "SUPERSEDED" list (S2 field→drift, S3 margins, S5 retest, S6 P_max/τ, S7 equilibrium pace)
is RESCINDED. Those are causal-dimension work (field/deployment, physiology sharpening), re-housed
under the Wave-2 causal-network plan. What IS closed: the expectation that any single dimension must
match form, and the form-as-measurement route. `journal/state/MODEL_STATE.md` updated accordingly.
