STANDING METHODOLOGICAL WARNING for any future session working this model.

The trap: when the model trails the market, defaulting to "the remaining gap is private/connections
information the market prices and we cannot see with public data, so public signals are tapped out."
This framing is DEFEATIST and has been empirically FALSIFIED TWICE:
  - FORM (leak-free relative-finish ability): lifted the model 11% -> 29% of market skill.
  - JOCKEY (trailing strike rate, coupled as an effective-v_c measurement): 29% -> ~38%, P=99.2%.
Both are PUBLIC data. Each gain came from correct FEATURISATION + STRUCTURE, not new private info.

The correct stance:
  - The gap is a FEATURISATION/STRUCTURE problem, not an information ceiling. Target >=90% of market on
    public data is considered very doable (owner's standing belief). The work is not meant to be easy;
    hitting walls is the NORM, not a stop signal.
  - Wins hide in STRUCTURE, not in piling on covariates. The ABM (interacting traffic / power-balance EOM)
    was an owner-introduced structure the agent did NOT find on its own — a reminder to think structurally.
  - When you catch yourself about to write "market prices private info" / "public signals exhausted" /
    "no edge left": STOP. That is the anti-pattern. Instead hunt: (a) sharper featurisation of the
    dominant signals (e.g. margin/time-based speed-figure form vs coarse position-rank form), (b)
    conditions-conditional ability (distance/going/course/class suitability), (c) EMERGENT field structure
    the ABM uniquely expresses (pace/race-shape from the field style composition), (d) unscraped public
    sources (barrier trials, drone-CV trajectories).
  - "No more defeatism" is a direct standing instruction from the owner.
