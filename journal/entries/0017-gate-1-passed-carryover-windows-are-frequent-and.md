Backfill research (Racing News + SCMP). Triple Trio (3 trios) and Six-Up Six Win Bonus roll over FREQUENTLY (the hard pools go unwon more often than won; Double Trio is usually won/small). Fresh TT NET pools collapsed ~HK$10M->~HK$2M while carried jackpots routinely sit HK$3-30M (rare HK$60-100M, capped at HK$100M since 1997). So C/G on carryover meetings is regularly >> 0.15, often >=1 -> structurally +EV windows exist, plausibly dozens/season. KEY REFINEMENT: chase money floods PUBLICIZED big jackpots and erodes the edge, so the cleaner edge is likely in MODEST, under-publicized carryovers (small HK$2-5M TT rolls), NOT the headline HK$60M ones. Gate-2 constraints: 17-19% takeout, the HK$100M cap, chase dilution, combinatorial cover cost.
