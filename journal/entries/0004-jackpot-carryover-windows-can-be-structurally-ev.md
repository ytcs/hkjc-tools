Double Trio, Triple Trio and Six Up carry an un-won jackpot forward (~7.5% jackpot reserve + 0.5% operator skim). On a carryover meeting the pool can pay out more than is bet that day => effective takeout on new money goes NEGATIVE, independent of any modelling skill. Worth a dedicated mechanical detector; size the buy as a Flexi-unit combinatorial cover, not a single bet.
