STAGE A LUT (finish-cam, vid t=102.5-123.0s where the feed cuts to a side-on finish tracking camera with legible saddlecloth numbers): RapidOCR dual-scale (1.6x+2.2x), conf>0.45, pooled, >=2-hit gate => 8/12 numbers (#1,2,3,5,6,8,9,12; back-markers #4,7,10,11 exit the finish-cam frame, never OCR'd at any threshold). For each OCR'd number the nearest saturated silk blob ABOVE it (jockey torso) contributes a SAT-WEIGHTED HUE HISTOGRAM (18 bins). KEY METHOD POINT: a LAB/BGR median collapses every silk to muddy red on these overlapping low-res side-on silks (the partial result 0081); a hue histogram keeps each silk's distinct hues, so blue (#5,#6), yellow (#1,#2-cream) and navy (#3) separate from the red/pink cluster.
STAGE B head-on signatures: re-sample every detected jockey's silk hue histogram (the SAME _silk_mask/detect_jockeys machinery as cv_patrol_lateral) per frame, tag by (off_m,depth) from the 0076 homography, bin to each robust track, mean-histogram per track => 12/12 robust tracks signed.
ASSIGN: Hungarian one-to-one on a circular hue-histogram EMD (NOT rotation-invariant — silks are absolute hues), track<->LUT. Confidence = margin of the assigned cost to the track's 2nd-best, scaled by the field-median margin; conf<0.35 flagged AMBIGUOUS. Result: 8 tracks numbered; colour-CONFIDENT #2,#5,#8,#9 (the distinct yellow/blue/pink-red silks), AMBIGUOUS #1,#3,#6,#12 (the red/pink/magenta family that is genuinely degenerate at 1080p side-on — verified by eye: #8 navy+pink, #12 white+pink, #2 cream, #9 red, #6 blue, #3 navy+red).
