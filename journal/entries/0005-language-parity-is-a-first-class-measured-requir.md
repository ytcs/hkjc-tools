A large share of informed HK racing opinion exists only in Chinese (zh-hk). Reading English well and Chinese poorly = a systematic blind spot exactly where alpha lives. Every DataSource carries a `languages` field and the harness reports lift broken down by source language, so a Chinese gap shows up as a number rather than hiding. Frontier multilingual reasoning is the advantage to exploit here, not a chore to skip.
