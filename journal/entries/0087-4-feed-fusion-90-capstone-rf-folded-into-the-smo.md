THE INTEGRATION (the modelling crux). Kept the 0084 lateral machinery AS-IS (per-horse numpy Kalman fwd-filter + RTS smoother, latent z=[s,s_dot,l,l_dot], 5Hz; bounded long. accel sigma0.8 + lat accel sigma0.35 motion prior applied jointly; aerial/drone/patrol lateral obs unchanged). FIXED s_h(t) by adding RF as obs-model 4, two ways:
- RF finish MARGINS -> METRIC longitudinal obs at the line (race_t 98.85): arclen = 1650 - margin_len*2.4, sd floored at RF MAE ~1.0m. 5 horses {3,6,8,9,12}. Pins the absolute per-horse SPREAD at the finish.
- RF legend ORDER -> per-horse arclen OFFSET from the FIELD-LEADER backbone reproducing the RF rank: obs = s_field(race_t) + (rank_mid - rank_h)*RANK_GAP(2.4m), sd 1.8m (tight vs gap => imposes ORDER; loose in absolute => honest mid-race metres). KEY ADDITION beyond the brief: the legend is the LEAD-GROUP strip (6-8 visible), so horses ABSENT from it at time t are BEHIND the deepest visible rank -> a soft back-marker obs (rank=max+1, sd2.5) stops back-markers floating to the field mean (THE corruption that otherwise capped the per-pole rho at ~0.6). Plus a finish-LINE order carry-forward (last-late-legend rank -> arclen behind the winner) for legend-seen horses RF gives no metric margin, and a back-of-field placement for the never-late no-margin horses (#1/#7). Also had to LOOSEN the aerial FIELD-odometer arclen sd 3->7m: it is the field-MEAN (shared across horses), and its hundreds of identical per-row obs were clamping every horse to the field mean and erasing the RF stagger.

RESULTS — the LONGITUDINAL axis is now MEANINGFUL (the 0084 gap CLOSED):
- per-pole fused lead-order vs official RP: rho +0.96 / +0.80 / +0.90 / +0.71 at the 1/4,1/2,3/4,finish calls. 0084 was rho ~0 (-0.57/-0.56/+0.31/+0.03). A decisive jump toward RF own +0.98..+1.00 at the poles; the 1/2 pole (+0.80) is the honest soft spot (the deepest-visible vs first-absent legend boundary is fuzzy there).
- fused FINISH ORDER vs official: Spearman rho +0.68; winner #3 RECOVERED (fused leader = #3); top-2 exact (#3,#12). Fused [3,12,5,2,6,8,9,4,10,11,1,7] vs official [3,12,6,2,9,8,11,4,7,10,5,1].
