3. Scorer (0272 / 0285 conventions). t_off: full official times at 800/600/400/200/0 m to go (sub-splits from
   official_subsplits on the full record). t_c: first crossing of the product (paths.parquet), linear in s; v there.
   Half a->b: ot = t_off(b) - t_off(a), dT = t_c(b) - t_c(a) - ot, frac = |dT - median over race-half dT| / ot, kept when
   ot > 5 s and >= 3 horses in the race-half.
- P1 (primary): p90 frac over the halves bracketing the withheld point (k = 0: 800-600, 600-400; k = 1: 400-200, 200-0),
  on arm from on_Sk, off arm from off_U, pooled over k. Paired on - off, race-bootstrap 90 % CI (2000, seed 0).
- P2 (primary): error at the withheld point, e = (t_c - t_off) * v (m), e_rel = e - race-point median (>= 3 horses);
  p90 |e_rel| pooled over k, same pairing and CI. Median |e_rel| reported.
- Scorer check (off arm, before reading any on number): P1-type p90 over all four halves of off_U within 0.3 pp of 1.18 %,
  and SD of e_rel at sub-split points within 1 m of 2.1 m; else stop and fix the scorer.
